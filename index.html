A snack with its own character.

Boomstick is inspired by lumpia, combining a savory filling with chili, wrapped and cooked to create a snack with a spicy twist.

Our product offers a different way to enjoy familiar snack flavors. It is made for people looking for something flavorful to enjoy during snack time.
