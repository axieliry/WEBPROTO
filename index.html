PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

Product Testing

Product testing helped us identify a problem with Boomstick's spiciness and showed us what needed improvement during preparation.

🧪

01 / THE ACTIVITY

Customers Tried Boomstick

Customers tried our product and shared their feedback about its taste and spiciness. Their comments helped us identify an issue with the way the peppers were prepared.

💬

02 / CUSTOMER FEEDBACK

The Product Was Not Spicy Enough

Customers noticed that Boomstick did not have the level of spiciness they expected. This feedback led us to look more closely at the preparation of the peppers.

01

Spiciness

The product lacked the expected spicy kick.

02

Preparation

The way the peppers were prepared needed attention.

03

Improvement

The preparation process needed to be reviewed.

🔎

03 / PROBLEM IDENTIFIED

The Insides of the Peppers Were Washed

We discovered that we had accidentally washed the insides of the peppers. This affected the spiciness of Boomstick and helped explain why customers found it less spicy than expected.

What went wrong?

Accidentally washing the insides of the peppers affected the spicy result we wanted for our product.

💡

04 / LESSON LEARNED

Improving Our Preparation

We learned that proper pepper preparation is important to achieving the expected spiciness. For future batches, we need to take greater care when preparing the peppers and check the spice level of the finished product.

Our next step

Pay closer attention when preparing the peppers and evaluate the spiciness before serving the product.
